Vision Transformer accelerator on Zynq UltraScale+

Xilinx ZCU104 · Vitis HLS · Vitis AI DPU · AXI · May 2026

50.7× faster than the on-board ARM Cortex-A53 baseline, end to end.

Softmax, GELU and LayerNorm are the parts of a transformer the DPU can't run well, so they became custom fully pipelined HLS IP on the programmable logic, talking to the processor over AXI. Matrix multiplies stay on the DPU.

  • Timing closed at 300 MHz with PIPELINE/DATAFLOW pragmas and interface tuning.
  • DeiT-Small inference: 72.5 ms measured on the board vs 3671 ms on the CPU, at ~15 W.

Asynchronous FIFO, verified with UVM

Verilog RTL · SystemVerilog · UVM 1.2 · SVA · Apr 2026

Clock-domain crossing done right and proven with a full testbench, not a directed test.

Gray-coded read and write pointers cross domains through two-stage synchronisers, so full and empty flags are safe under any clock ratio. The point of the project was the verification side.

  • UVM environment: sequencer, driver, monitor on both ports, reference-queue scoreboard.
  • Constrained-random stimulus, SystemVerilog assertions on pointer and flag behaviour, cross coverage of simultaneous read/write.
  • 500-transaction random regression plus directed overflow and underflow tests, all passing.

Adaptive ROI video pipeline on the edge

Xilinx ZCU104 · Vitis AI DPU (YOLOv4) · Video Codec Unit · May 2026

Detect, prioritise, encode entirely on the device, with no host in the loop.

YOLOv4 on the DPU finds what matters in each frame; a three-zone region-of-interest mask steers quality toward it; the hardware VCU encodes H.264 at the output. A surveillance pipeline that works without a server.
